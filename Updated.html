Dear Internet Explorer,

I’m writing this because we need to move on, and frankly, you’ve been lagging behind for a decade. While I’ll always appreciate you being my first gateway to the World Wide Web, our relationship has become a series of "Not Responding" windows and forced restarts. I need a partner who can keep up with the modern pace of life, not one that treats a simple CSS rendering like a complex architectural feat. It’s not that I’ve changed; it’s that the rest of the world updated, and you stayed frozen in 2009.

To be clear, this isn't a snap decision. I’ve spent a lot of time analyzing our performance metrics, and the syllabus of our relationship just isn't meeting the required learning outcomes. Here are the primary reasons why I’m opting for a cleaner uninstall:
  1. Total Lack of Agility: Your "Not Responding" status has become your personality trait.
  2. Security Vulnerabilities: You let every bad influence through the door without a second thought.
  3. Compatibility Issues: Modern sites don't even recognize you anymore; you’ve become a ghost in your own machine.
  4. The "Edge" Factor: Your younger sibling is just... more capable, faster, and actually understands the assignment.

Consider this a final lecture on the evolution of technology: survival depends on adaptation, and you’ve reached your logical "End of Life" phase. You taught me patience—mostly because I had no choice while waiting for your progress bars to move—but I’ve graduated to a browser that doesn't crash when I open more than three tabs. We had a good run back in the dial-up days, but I’m clearing my cache and moving toward a faster future. 
Sincerely,

A Former User
